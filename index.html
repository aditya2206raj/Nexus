About CivicConnect

Connecting Darbhanga's citizens with faster, more transparent civic services.

See an issue. Report it. Track it. Get it resolved.

CivicConnect is a civic-tech platform built for Darbhanga, Bihar. It gives citizens a simple, transparent way to report public problems — potholes, broken streetlights, garbage, water leaks, drainage issues and more — with photo evidence and location details, while helping local authorities review, prioritize, assign and resolve complaints efficiently.

🎯 Our Mission

Reduce the gap between citizens reporting problems and authorities resolving them, through a transparent digital bridge.

🏗️ Built For Darbhanga

Designed around real Darbhanga localities — Laheriasarai, Tower Chowk, Donar, Benta, Kadirabad and more.

🔍 Full Transparency

Every complaint moves through a public status timeline: Reported, Verified, Assigned, In Progress, Resolved, Closed.

Hackathon Prototype

CivicConnect is a demonstration prototype built for a hackathon. It uses mock data stored in your browser's local storage and does not connect to a real backend, database or government system. It is not officially affiliated with the Darbhanga Municipal Corporation or any government authority. All statistics, complaints and resolution records shown are for demonstration purposes only.
